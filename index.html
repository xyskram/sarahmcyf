Football

How it began

I played football as a child at school, but then didn't play again until my late 30s when I decided I wanted to try playing. I joined a 9 a side team and absolutely loved it, however there were not a lot of games in the season in 9 a side, so the following season I started with an 11 a side team.

11-a-Side

I am currently playing for an 11-a-side team, and enjoy it hugely. It's helped to massively improve my fitness, and also my confidence. I tend to play on the wing, but here is a list of the positions I have played for the team

  • Right Wing
  • Left Wing
  • Striker
  • Centre Mid
  • Right back (when we had 2 players sent off in a game!)
And this is the same list in order of my favourite to least favourite position -
  1. Right Wing
  2. Striker
  3. Left Wing
  4. Centre Mid
  5. Right Back
